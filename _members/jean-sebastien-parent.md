Jean-Sébastien Parent is a Research Scientist with Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada in Ottawa. His work explores the molecular mechanisms that shape plant growth and reproduction, with an emphasis on small RNA biology, gene regulation, and crop genetics. After earning a Ph.D. in Biochemistry from the Université de Montréal, he pursued postdoctoral research at INRAE Versailles and Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory. His research team integrates molecular biology, genomics, and bioinformatics to uncover genetic factors that influence important agricultural traits and to support the development of improved crop varieties.
