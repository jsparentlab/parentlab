# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %}Research

### Reproductive Development

Our research examines the molecular and developmental processes that govern reproduction in agriculturally important crop species. 
We investigate key stages of plant reproduction, including floral architecture, fertilization, and seed development, with the goal of understanding how these processes influence crop performance and yield. 
In particular, we study the roles of microRNAs, DNA methylation, and other genetic and epigenetic factors in regulating reproductive development and shaping plant productivity.

### RNA-Based Disease Control

The development of RNA-based crop protection strategies offers a promising alternative to conventional chemical pesticides. 
Small RNA formulations have the potential to control current and emerging plant pests and pathogens while minimizing impacts on beneficial organisms and biodiversity. 
In collaboration with academic and industry partners, we identify essential genetic targets in pests, design and optimize RNA molecules, and evaluate their effectiveness and safety. 
Through this work, we aim to develop sustainable technologies that support resilient and environmentally responsible agriculture.
